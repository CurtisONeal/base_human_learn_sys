## Transcript quality — names may be misheard

The transcript was produced by machine speech recognition ({{PROVENANCE}}),
not typed by a human. A deterministic pass has already fixed the known
mishearings it can recognize safely, but product and proper names may still be
misheard — for example a bare "cloud" or "claw" where the speaker clearly meant
"Claude", or "cloud code" for "Claude Code". When the context makes the
intended name clear, write the correct name in your notes (and list the line
under Transcription Uncertainty). Do not "correct" a word that is right in
context ("cloud provider", "AWS cloud").
